The planned research workflow

Start broad.
Keep the evidence.

A useful literature collection starts with discovery and stays connected to its sources. This is the workflow we are building toward.

  1. 01

    Search with recall in mind

    Retrieve scholarly metadata through permitted APIs and other authorized sources. Coverage will depend on each source’s scope, access conditions and limits.

  2. 02

    Query an inspectable collection

    Normalize identifiers, merge duplicate records and retain source URLs and retrieval context. Keep conflicting metadata visible for review.

  3. 03

    Screen after retrieval, when useful

    Add optional relevance screening after broad retrieval. Preserve the retrieved set and screening decisions so researchers can revisit what was excluded.

Metadata discovery does not grant access to full text. Any full-text retrieval will depend on open access, your entitlements and the source’s terms. Researchers must check the original work before relying on an answer.
